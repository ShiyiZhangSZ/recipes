# Egg with Asparagus
Serving Size: 1
## Ingredients
* 2-3 large eggs
* 1/2 pounds asparagus
* salt
* black pepper
* parmigiano reggiano (optional)
* 15 ml milk (optional)
## Instructions
* Start with boiling a pot of water and salt your water before preparing the asparagus. 
* For asparagus, you can go either way, trimming the thick ends directly, or just peeling the outer skin of the lower half. 
* Slice the asparagus 2 inches per piece (45 diagonal cuts are better)
* Blanch the asparagus for 1 minute and transfer it to the ice bath
* For eggs, whisk 3 large eggs with salt and black pepper (you can add the 15 ml milk or any plant-based alternative, or nothing based on your preference)
* Heat your pan with medium heat. Add any oil with high smoke point (if you whisk thoroughly and the pan is hot enough, the egg is not likely to stick on the pan even with less oil). Let it sit for 10 seconds and gently scramble it until the egg is half cooked, remove it from the pan.
* Add more oil (if you have a lot in the previous step you can use the left oil on the pan). Stir fry the asparagus with seasonings (add salt if you just want a classic breakfast dish, you can also swipe it with oyster sauce, which gives the dish extra umami flavor and a touch of sweetness. 
* Pour the egg back and stir-fry them together quickly and done.

## Variation
* if you use salt + black pepper for seasoning the dish, shave some parmesean at the end. 
* if I have some shrimps in my fridge, I will chop them into cubes and stir fry with the asparagus and oyster sauce. 
* You can also do the egg dish in a frittata style or serve it as soft-boiled jammy eggs. Eggs and asparagus are a great pair.
